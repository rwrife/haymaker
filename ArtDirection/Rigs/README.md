# Fighter rigs

The ten boxer sculpts (`ArtDirection/Models/<name>.obj`: mack, bruiser, goldie, nico, atlas, duke, sylvie,
cassian, vera, jaison) share one Mixamo-compatible 22-bone skeleton,
so every Mixamo boxing clip in `Haymaker/Models/fighter.clips` plays on every character. This folder
holds the rig sources. The runtime assets `Haymaker/Models/<name>.fighter` (HMR2) are packed from them.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `mixamo-reference-skeleton.json` | Mixamo's own joint fit for Mack (from the FBX rest pose). It is the calibration reference. |
| `<name>.skin` | HMS1 skin sidecar: fitted joints plus per-vertex bone weights, in OBJ vertex order. Generated by Blender and committed. |
| `<name>.joints.json` | Human-readable dump of the same joints, the measured landmarks (`notes`) and the bone-heat report. |

## Pipeline

```
ArtDirection/Models/<name>.obj ──(Blender, scripts/blender/autorig.py)──▶ ArtDirection/Rigs/<name>.skin (+ .joints.json)
ArtDirection/Models/<name>.obj + <name>.skin ──(python3 scripts/rig_fighters.py)──▶ Haymaker/Models/<name>.fighter
```

Run both steps from the repo root:

```sh
# 1. Fit joints and compute weights (headless Blender 5.x; about 15 s for all ten).
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
    --python scripts/blender/autorig.py -- [mack bruiser ...] [--out ArtDirection/Rigs] [--fbx-dir ~/Downloads]

# 2. Pack the runtime assets (system python3, stdlib only, no Blender).
python3 scripts/rig_fighters.py [names] [--output Haymaker/Models] [--rigs ArtDirection/Rigs]

# 3. Check (also runs in CI).
python3 scripts/test_rig_fighters.py
```

- With no names, both scripts process all ten boxers (`NAMES` in `scripts/rig_fighters.py` and
  `scripts/blender/autorig_common.py`; add new fighters to both).
- Both steps are deterministic: re-running them produces byte-identical files.
- CI does not run Blender. It re-packs every `.fighter` from the committed OBJ and `.skin` and requires a
  byte-for-byte match. **Whenever an OBJ changes, re-run step 1 for that boxer and then step 2**; the packer
  refuses a sidecar whose vertex count doesn't match its OBJ.
- `--fbx-dir` is optional. If `<fbx-dir>/Idle.fbx` exists, autorig checks `mixamo-reference-skeleton.json`
  against the Mixamo rest pose in it. Mixamo FBX files are licensed assets: never copy them into the repo.

### 1. Joint fitting (`scripts/blender/autorig_joints.py`)

Conventions are Mixamo's: Y-up, +Z front, and `Left*` bones on the character's anatomical left (+X).

- **Mack** is the calibration subject. Mixamo auto-rigged `mack.obj`, so his spine, arms and head use the
  reference skeleton exactly.
  - His legs are the exception. Mixamo put the knees, ankles and toes on the inner surface of the legs,
    so they are re-centred in the limb cross-sections. Heights and depths are kept.
  - Mack's fitted values: LeftLeg x .082, LeftFoot x .117, LeftToeBase (.143, .007, .045).
- **The other nine** use the same landmarks, measured on each sculpt. Joints are placed at the same
  relative positions as on Mack and centred in the limb cross-sections. Each side is fitted separately.
  - Landmarks: shoulder line, armpit, glove cuff, glove tip, crotch, chin, boot heel/sole/toe and mesh top.
  - The elbow and wrist sit at Mack's fractions of shoulder→cuff and cuff→glove tip. The hand aims at
    the glove tip along the forearm.
  - Ankle = top of the boot. Feet and toes are aimed along the Mixamo source directions so soles stay
    flat after retargeting.
  - Glove cuff: by default, the first arm slab whose colours differ from the shoulder-area skin. Jaison's
    low-poly sculpt (4.8k vertices) has noisy baked-texture skin that trips this test on the bare forearm.
    When the test fires at the very start of the search, the cuff is instead where the slab's median colour
    leaves the mid-forearm colour. That puts his cuff at x .35 (left) and .335 (right); the white cuff rim
    is at about .345–.35. These were measured on the supplied colours, before his gloves were repainted
    (white cuff from |x| .342, black glove from .388; see `ArtDirection/Models/README.md`).
- `<name>.joints.json → notes` records the measured landmarks for review.

### 2. Bone heat (`scripts/blender/autorig_weights.py`)

The Tripo sculpts are non-manifold, so Blender's bone heat (`ARMATURE_AUTO`) fails on them directly.
Instead:

1. The sculpt is voxel-remeshed into a watertight proxy (voxel = 0.006 × height, holes filled).
2. Bone heat runs on the proxy. Mixamo's Neck bone is about 1 mm long, so for heating only, the neck
   segment is extended 35% of the way up the head.
3. The weights are transferred back to every sculpt vertex by barycentric interpolation at the nearest
   proxy surface point.
4. Heat failure is detected numerically:
   - If a bone is left empty, or more than 2% of vertices get no weight, the whole model falls back to
     inverse-distance-to-bone weights.
   - If only a few vertices get no weight, just those vertices fall back.
   - `joints.json → heat` records which case applied (`fallback: none` for all ten at present).

### 3. Anatomical post-processing (`scripts/blender/autorig_regions.py`)

Heat is kept where it is good (the torso gradient, deltoids, hips and trunks). It is replaced by analytic
weights where the rig must be exact. All distances are fractions of the sculpt height `h`; the constants are
at the top of the file.

| Region | Rule | Constant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sides | A vertex never carries weight from the other side's arm or leg bones. | — |
| Gloves | Everything past the cuff plane is rigid `*Hand`. ForeArm→Hand blends only on the bare wrist before the cuff. | `WRIST_BAND` .028 |
| Elbow | Arm/ForeArm blend across the elbow bisector plane. | `ELBOW_BAND` .024 |
| Pelvis | UpLeg ramps in along the thigh axis below the hip joint. Trunks above it blend with Hips, so the waistband doesn't follow a lifted thigh. | — |
| Crotch | Leg weight fades into Hips near the mid-plane. | `CROTCH_BAND` .040 |
| Knee | UpLeg/Leg band. | `KNEE_BAND` .030 |
| Ankle | Leg/Foot band, perpendicular to the shin. The boot (heel included) is rigid `*Foot`. | `ANKLE_BAND` (.022 above, .012 below) |
| Ball of foot | Foot/ToeBase band; the toe cap is rigid `*ToeBase`. | `BALL_BAND` .014 |
| Clavicles | `*Shoulder` is cleared from the neck, sternum and spine (it would collapse the neck when both arms go up). | — |
| Neck | Inside the neck cylinder, Neck+Head weight is spread by height from the neck base to the jaw. | — |
| Head | Rigid `Head` above a jaw plane, within the head's measured half-width. | `JAW_BAND` .014 |
| Trapezius | Behind and beside the head, Head weight fades with distance from the rigid head (for the neckless boxers). | `TRAP_FALLOFF` .07 |
| Smoothing | Laplacian smoothing over mesh edges; the neck/traps get extra passes, with the rigid head and arms pinned. | `SMOOTH_ITERS` 6, `NECK_SMOOTH_ITERS` 16 |
| Final | Top 4 influences, descending, weights < 0.01 pruned, renormalised. | `PRUNE` .01 |

## HMS1 skin sidecar (`<name>.skin`)

The file is little-endian, with every value in OBJ units (un-normalised).

| Offset | Layout | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `char[4] "HMS1"`, `u32 vertexCount`, `u32 boneCount` (= 22), `u32 reserved` (= 0) | header |
| 16 | 22 × `{f32 head[3], f32 aim[3]}` | joint head and aim point per bone, in skeleton order |
| 544 | vertexCount × `{u8 bone[4], u16 weight[4]}` | 12 bytes per vertex, in OBJ vertex order |

- `vertexCount` equals the number of `v` lines in the OBJ.
- Weights are sorted descending and sum to exactly 65535.
- Unused lanes are bone 0 with weight 0.
- `aim` is the child joint (or the end point for Head, Hands and ToeBases), so the bone direction is
  `aim − head`.

## HMR2 runtime asset (`Haymaker/Models/<name>.fighter`)

`scripts/rig_fighters.py` packs the OBJ and its sidecar. The authoritative layout is the HMR2 contract
consumed by the Godot loader (`godot/scripts/arena/fighter_assets.gd`). The generator currently stages files in `Haymaker/Models/`; copy them into `godot/assets/models/` for runtime use. In summary:

```
char[4] "HMR2", u32 vertexCount, u32 indexCount, u32 boneCount (= 22)
22 × { i32 parent, f32 head[3], f32 aim[3] }                     (runtime units)
vertexCount × { f32 pos[3], f32 normal[3], f32 rgb[3], f32 weight[4], u16 bone[4] }   (60 bytes)
indexCount × u32                                                 (triangles)
```

- Positions and joints are normalised to runtime units: `v / sculpt height × 2.7`.
- Faces are fan-triangulated. Normals are area-weighted smooth normals. Colours are the OBJ vertex colours.
- Skin weights are `u16 / 65535`.

## Tests (`scripts/test_rig_fighters.py`)

The tests use the stdlib only (python3.9+) and run in about 2 s. They check:

- Every `.fighter` is byte-identical to a fresh pack.
- Header, sizes, bone names and the parent table match the contract. Bones are non-degenerate and
  `Left*` limbs are at +X.
- Vertex data: attributes are finite, normals unit length, weights sum to 1, sorted, bone ids < 22.
- Gloves: every vertex past the cuff is ≥ 0.98 Hand.
- No left-limb weight on the right half, or vice versa.
- Boots: below the ankle, only same-side Leg/Foot/ToeBase.
- Head: the face and skull carry only `Head`.

## QA

Weights were tuned against the Mixamo clips, retargeted with the contract's math (Δ·C per bone, hip height
scaled). The checks were rendered contact sheets of the extreme frames for all ten boxers, plus per-edge
stretch statistics:

- Glove, boot and toe-cap edges keep their length exactly (stretch 1.000) in every clip.
- Knees, elbows and ankles stay at or below about 2.5× local stretch.
- The largest remaining stretch (about 5×) is the inner thigh at the crotch in the deepest *Falling Down*
  frame, where the thigh is flexed about 106°. This is the normal linear-blend-skinning limit.
- Jaison's low-poly sculpt has the highest shoulder stretch: about 5.4× on 5 mm edges of the torn-sleeve
  ruffles and vest shoulder in *Left Block*. It is not visible in the renders.
