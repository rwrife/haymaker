Haymaker — Offline Arcade Boxing for iPhone

Step into a boss-rush boxing game built for one-handed play. Each original opponent has a distinct rhythm and visible tells. Watch the windup, dodge or guard, and time your counter.

WHAT YOU DO
- Work through a career ladder of eight original opponents, from Goldie Voltage to final boss Bruiser Baxter. Each opponent has an authored move pattern.
- Tap to jab and swipe for a hook, uppercut, dodge, or guard. Prefer buttons? Switch to the two-handed control layout.
- Chase personal bests. Bout scores use landed punches, defense, damage, rounds, and time; records are derived from locally saved bouts.
- Play Endless Sparring against a chosen opponent and track the rounds in your run.

BUILT AROUND THE FIGHT
- Seeded fights follow the same pattern when given the same inputs. There are no random fight outcomes, energy timers, or pay-to-progress mechanics.
- Original 3D fighters face off in an arena rendered on your iPhone.
- No account, cloud save, multiplayer, ads, in-app purchases, loot boxes, or network connection is needed for play.

YOUR CORNER
- Career progress, bout records, and control settings stay on your iPhone.
- Settings include high-contrast tell cues, reduced motion, and a haptics toggle. Match controls and menus have accessibility labels.

Haymaker is a game, not a fitness or medical program. Its fighters and artwork are original; it does not depict real boxers or boxing franchises.
