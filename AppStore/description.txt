Haymaker — Offline Arcade Boxing for iPhone and iPad

Face a boss-rush of original fighters. Read each opponent's windup, dodge or guard, and time your counter. Play one-handed with taps and swipes, or choose a two-handed button layout.

WHAT YOU DO
- Fight through a career ladder of ten original opponents. The first nine change order each ladder; Bruiser Baxter waits at the end.
- Tap for a jab; swipe for a hook, uppercut, dodge, or guard. Practice against a chosen opponent in sparring rounds.
- Review saved bouts and personal bests on the records screen. Scores account for landed punches, defense, damage, rounds, and time.

BUILT AROUND THE FIGHT
- Seeded fights give the same result for the same inputs. There are no energy timers or pay-to-progress mechanics.
- Original 3D fighters face off in a Metal-rendered arena.
- Play offline without an account, cloud save, multiplayer, ads, in-app purchases, or loot boxes.

YOUR CORNER
- Career progress, bout records, and control settings stay on your device.
- Settings include high-contrast tell cues, reduced motion, and a haptics toggle. Match controls and menus have accessibility labels.

Haymaker is a game, not a fitness or medical program. Its fighters and artwork are original; it does not depict real boxers or boxing franchises.
